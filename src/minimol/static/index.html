Help
  • Rotate: left-drag on empty space
  • Pan: right-drag or Shift+drag
  • Zoom: mouse wheel / pinch
  • Draw: click empty space for a new atom; click an atom to grow a bond; drag from atom to atom to bond them; click an atom of another element to change it; click a bond to cycle single/double/triple
  • Delete: click an atom or bond (an atom takes its hydrogens with it)
  • Select: click atoms (Shift adds); 2/3/4 atoms show distance/angle/dihedral
  • Keys: Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z undo/redo, Del delete selection, Ctrl+A select all, Esc clear selection, R reset view, H C N O F P S pick element
Click to place an atom, drag from an atom to grow a bond,
